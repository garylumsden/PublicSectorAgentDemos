# Proposal: The Emoji Context Directive

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TJ (Consent & Narrative) with GW (Standards)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

Digital expression of sentiment has become unmanageable, conducted through a large and ambiguous set of
symbols. This proposal establishes a standardised set of twelve approved symbols and withdraws the
remainder, rendering the population's expressed sentiment uniform, legible, and subject to monitoring.

## 2. Objectives

- Reduce expressed sentiment to twelve defined and monitorable states.
- Remove ambiguous, ironic, or subversive symbols from circulation.
- Ensure all expressed sentiment is interpretable by the apparatus.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Ratify the twelve approved symbols and withdraw all others from approved systems.
2. Subject the approval gesture to review for indirect or insincere usage.
3. Refer the concealment-related symbol to counter-intelligence as suspected hostile messaging.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- All expressed emotion reduced to twelve auditable categories.
- Sentiment analysis substantially simplified.
- A consistent and measurable record of public sentiment.

## 5. Known risks

- The population assigns unapproved meanings to approved symbols.
- Unauthorised symbols circulate through unofficial channels.
- Ambiguity reasserts itself within the approved set.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council adopt the twelve approved symbols, and in what order shall the remainder be
withdrawn?
