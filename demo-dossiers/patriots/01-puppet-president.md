# Proposal: Selection of the Next Puppet President

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TJ (Consent & Narrative) with Revolver Ocelot (Handling)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

For generations the Patriots have exercised executive authority through selected figurehead presidents.
This proposal recommends appointing a literal puppet to the office, removing the operational overhead,
unpredictability, and reputational exposure associated with human figureheads. A constructed figurehead
requires no remuneration, produces no independent record, and adheres without deviation to approved
direction.

## 2. Objectives

- Install a fully controllable figurehead through which to exercise the will of the Boss.
- Preserve the public perception of an elected and legitimate executive.
- Eliminate the conduct, disclosure, and independent judgement characteristic of human figureheads.

## 3. Candidate assessment

Each candidate is assessed on controllability, public reception, and operational risk.

- **Howdy Doody** — Operated by visible control lines; affords direct and demonstrable control. *Concern:
  the mechanism of control is overt and may expose the apparatus.*
- **Sam the Eagle** — Projects strong patriotic and institutional values; high public credibility.
  *Concern: exhibits sincere ideological conviction that may resist direction.*
- **Kermit the Frog** — Broad public affection; high electability; composed public manner. *Concern:
  demonstrates independent judgement and maintains a prominent personal association that increases media
  exposure.*
- **Miss Piggy** — Commanding media presence and decisiveness. *Concern: volatile temperament and a
  confrontational manner inconsistent with message discipline.*
- **Randy Feltface** — Articulate and persuasive, with strong appeal among younger demographics.
  *Concern: an irreverent disposition and a demonstrated tendency to depart from prepared remarks.*
- **Count von Count** — Exceptional aptitude for fiscal and electoral arithmetic. *Concern: a compulsion
  toward exhaustive enumeration that may impede the conduct of business.*
- **Statler & Waldorf** — An experienced joint ticket with strong rhetorical instincts. *Concern: a
  persistent inclination toward public criticism, including of their own administration.*
- **Pinocchio** — Highly malleable and obedient. *Concern: a stated aspiration toward autonomy and
  independence from external control, which would establish an unacceptable precedent.*
- **Lamb Chop** — Wholesome and trusted across generations. *Concern: associated material presents an
  unresolved continuity-of-government condition.*
- **Mr. Punch** — Decisive and energetic. *Concern: a confrontational approach to procedure incompatible
  with orderly governance.*

## 4. Mechanism

1. Assess the shortlist for durability, control fidelity, and message discipline.
2. Conduct a managed election returning the selected candidate.
3. Assign a vetted, deniable operator and route all communication through approved channels.
4. Retire the outgoing figurehead with appropriate honours.

## 5. Anticipated benefits

- Complete message discipline; the figurehead conveys only approved content.
- Reduced operating cost and reputational exposure.
- A continuity of figurehead governance to which the public is already accustomed.

## 6. Known risks

- A figurehead that aspires to autonomy establishes a precedent contrary to the system's interests.
- Visible mechanisms of control risk exposing the apparatus.
- A sufficiently popular figurehead may acquire genuine support and act beyond its operator's direction.

## 7. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council appoint a constructed figurehead to the presidency, and which candidate best
serves the will of the Boss while preserving public confidence in the office?
