# Proposal: The Official History Revision Programme

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** GW (System Architecture) with Sigint (Records)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

The integrity of governance requires that the historical record remain consistent with current policy.
This proposal institutes a programme of continuous, undisclosed revision to the historical record,
correcting facts that have become inconsistent with present directives. The current revision retires
several such facts and standardises the documented position of a prominent national figure on a matter
of professional convention.

## 2. Objectives

- Maintain consistency between the historical record and current policy.
- Retire established facts that conflict with present directives.
- Conduct revision continuously and without disclosure.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Prepare revisions in a controlled secondary record and assess them against public recall in limited
   regions.
2. Deploy approved revisions across educational materials, archives, and reference systems.
3. Publish no record of changes; disclosure of the revision process constitutes an unacceptable exposure.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- A historical record permanently aligned with present policy.
- The capacity to reverse any revision found to attract attention.
- A single authoritative account maintained at all times.

## 5. Known risks

- A surviving primary source contradicts the revised record.
- Privately retained copies persist outside central control.
- Two inconsistent versions of the record enter public circulation simultaneously.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council adopt continuous revision of the historical record, and shall the current
revision proceed?
