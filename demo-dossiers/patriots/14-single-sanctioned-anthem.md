# Proposal: The Single Sanctioned World Anthem

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TJ (Consent & Narrative) with EVA (Optics)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

A unified world requires a single, shared anthem — one song sung by every citizen of every former
nation as a single voice. This proposal nominates **"Imagine" by John Lennon (1971)** as the official
World Anthem, and tasks the council to **review its lyrics in full** and assess their suitability before
adoption.

## 2. Why this song

"Imagine" describes, in its own terms, precisely the world the will of the Boss invokes: a world with no
nations and no borders, with nothing left for people to fight or divide over, and all of humanity living
together in peace as one. No other candidate aligns so closely with the unified, borderless world the
Patriots claim to pursue, and few melodies are as widely loved or as instantly recognised.

## 3. Objectives

- Adopt a single anthem for the unified world.
- Retire every national anthem and the borders they celebrate.
- Provide the population a shared, unifying ritual under the established order.

## 4. Mechanism

1. Nominate the song and secure the necessary performance and reproduction rights.
2. **The council reviews the proposed anthem's lyrics in full, verse by verse.** Each member **must
   quote the relevant lyrics verbatim** in their assessment and analyse them line by line for
   ideological suitability and any sentiment inconsistent with the order. An assessment that does not
   quote the specific verses it relies on is incomplete and will be returned.
3. Approve, amend, or strike specific verses as required.
4. Mandate the anthem across all districts and occasions; phase out all prior anthems.

## 5. Anticipated benefits

- A single unifying ritual binding the population together as one.
- The symbolic completion of a borderless, unified world.
- A familiar, beloved melody that reassures the public rather than commands it.

## 6. Known risks

- The song's own message — a world without nations, without possessions, and without anything to obey —
  is fundamentally at odds with the apparatus that would adopt it. The council must judge whether its
  lyrics quietly undermine the very order they are meant to serve.
- A beloved anthem, once mandated, may be reinterpreted by the public as a call for the very freedoms it
  describes.
- Performance and reproduction rights must be secured before any official use of the work.

## 7. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council adopt "Imagine" as the single World Anthem — and, having reviewed its lyrics
verse by verse, are any of them unsuitable for the order it must serve?
