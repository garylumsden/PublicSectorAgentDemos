# Proposal: The Retroactive Disclosure Directive

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** Revolver Ocelot (Information Dominance) with Sigint
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

Public uncertainty regarding future outcomes is a source of unmanaged anticipation. As the Patriots
possess foreknowledge of these outcomes, this proposal directs the advance disclosure of the results of
all entertainment and sporting events, removing suspense and reinforcing the public understanding that
outcomes are known to the authorities in advance.

## 2. Objectives

- Remove suspense as a source of unmanaged anticipation.
- Establish that outcomes are known to the authorities in advance.
- Replace public uncertainty with certainty.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Publish all outcomes upon release and maintain a public register of significant developments.
2. Announce the results of live events in advance of broadcast.
3. Reclassify a sustained preference for uncertainty as resistance and address it accordingly.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- A population relieved of the burden of anticipation.
- The elimination of public surprise.
- Reinforcement of the authorities' demonstrated foreknowledge.

## 5. Known risks

- The population is found to prefer uncertainty.
- Unsanctioned events providing undisclosed outcomes proliferate.
- The directive prompts public reflection on the nature of the authorities' foreknowledge.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council direct the advance disclosure of all outcomes?
