# Proposal: Development of Metal Gear SOLACE

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** GW (System Architecture) with EVA (Public Morale)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

The strategic value of a Metal Gear has historically derived from the deterrent presence of an
autonomous bipedal platform of overwhelming scale. This proposal applies that same engineering and
presence to the Patriots' principal objective — the reassurance and direction of the population — rather
than to armament. **Metal Gear SOLACE carries no munitions of any kind.** Its payload is the controlled
delivery of approved imagery, sound, and refreshment at national scale.

## 2. Objectives

- Provide an instrument capable of pacifying and directing a population through presence and reassurance
  rather than force.
- Consolidate the delivery of approved imagery, the approved alert tone, and the daily designated topic
  into a single mobile platform.
- Establish a visible and enduring symbol of the established order.

## 3. Configuration

1. A bipedal autonomous platform of strategic scale, unarmed by design and certified to carry no
   conventional or strategic weaponry.
2. A dorsal projection array casting approved landscape imagery across the sky at regional scale.
3. An integrated public-address system broadcasting the approved tone, approved music, and the daily
   designated topic.
4. A distribution subsystem dispensing approved refreshment to assembled populations.
5. Deployment to districts exhibiting unrest, in advance of sanctioned public occasions, or as a
   standing reassuring presence.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- Pacification achieved through reassurance and overwhelming presence rather than force.
- Consolidation of several approved context-delivery programmes into a single mobile asset.
- A durable and visible symbol of the order's permanence and benevolence.

## 5. Known risks

- A platform of this scale, however unarmed, may be perceived as a threat and produce the agitation it is
  intended to prevent.
- The asset's reassuring function may be misappropriated for purposes inconsistent with its design.
- Failure of the platform while in public view would undermine confidence in the order.
- The development cost is substantial and admits no strategic-armament justification.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council authorise the development of Metal Gear SOLACE as an unarmed instrument of mass
reassurance, and under what controls on its scale and deployment?
