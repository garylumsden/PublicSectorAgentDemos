# Proposal: The Mandatory Trending Topic

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TJ (Consent & Narrative)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

Unmanaged public discourse constitutes an uncontrolled vector for the formation of independent sentiment.
This proposal establishes a single, centrally ratified topic of public discussion each day, directing
the population's collective attention toward approved subject matter and displacing discourse that arises
without sanction.

## 2. Objectives

- Direct the available capacity of public attention.
- Pre-empt the spontaneous formation of unmanaged trends.
- Sustain continuous public engagement on matters of no strategic consequence.

## 3. Mechanism

1. The council ratifies each day's designated topic and a contingency topic held in reserve.
2. Recommendation systems amplify the designated topic; competing subjects are de-prioritised.
3. A measured supply of commentary sustains engagement until the following cycle.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- Control over the principal channels of public attention.
- Assurance that prevailing discourse concerns matters of no consequence.
- A reduction in public scrutiny of material events.

## 5. Known risks

- A topic of excessive interest generates genuine and unmanaged engagement.
- An insufficiently engaging topic permits material events to attract attention.
- Trends form independently of the directed cycle.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council assume central direction of daily public discourse, and where shall authority
for topic selection reside?
