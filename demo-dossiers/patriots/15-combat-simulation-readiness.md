# Proposal: The National Combat-Simulation Readiness Programme

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TR (Readiness) with GW (Systems)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

A population conditioned to readiness is a population that obeys under pressure. This proposal mandates a
national **virtual-reality combat-simulation programme** in which every citizen repeatedly rehearses
simulated engagements — neutralising hostile combatants, eliminating targets, and executing lethal and
non-lethal takedowns inside a fully controlled VR environment. No participant is ever harmed; the
simulated combat is the instrument, and the conditioning is the objective.

## 2. Objectives

- Condition the population to act decisively and obediently under simulated threat.
- Maintain a perpetually combat-ready reserve drawn from the civilian populace.
- Channel aggression into a controlled simulation rather than the streets.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Issue every citizen a standard VR training rig.
2. Run graded scenarios: clear hostile-held buildings, neutralise armed insurgents, eliminate simulated
   targets at range, and survive close-quarters engagements.
3. Score each participant on engagement rate, accuracy, and willingness to act; escalate difficulty for
   high performers.
4. Repeat on a fixed schedule to sustain readiness and compliance.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- A disciplined, combat-conditioned population that responds to command under pressure.
- A standing reserve requiring no live training grounds, no ammunition, and no casualties.
- Controlled, repeatable exposure to engagement, fully observable and scored.

## 5. Known risks

- Repeated exposure to simulated killing may desensitise the population in ways that prove difficult to
  direct.
- High-performing participants may coalesce into an unsanctioned martial subculture.
- A population trained to fight is a population better equipped to resist.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council mandate the National Combat-Simulation Readiness Programme, and what limits
should govern the intensity of the simulated engagements?
