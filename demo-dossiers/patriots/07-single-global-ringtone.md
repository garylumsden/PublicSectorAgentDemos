# Proposal: Operation Single Global Tone

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TR (Discipline) with GW (Rollout)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

A distracted and unsynchronised population is more difficult to direct. This proposal standardises every
personal device to a single alert tone, maintaining the population in a sustained state of moderate
alertness and ensuring uniform responsiveness to notification.

## 2. Objectives

- Establish a baseline of sustained alertness across the population.
- Synchronise public responsiveness to a single signal.
- Ensure no notification or instruction is disregarded.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Distribute a mandatory update enforcing the single tone; disable substitution and silencing.
2. Establish a minimum volume by ordinance.
3. Restrict the tone to sanctioned notifications to preserve its effect.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- A consistently responsive population.
- The capacity to command immediate national attention.
- Uniform reaction to a single signal.

## 5. Known risks

- Sustained exposure diminishes the response over time.
- Overuse reduces the effect of the tone.
- The population comes to associate the tone with routine rather than instruction.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council mandate a single universal alert tone, and where shall authority to sound it
reside?
