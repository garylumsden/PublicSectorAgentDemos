# Proposal: The Single Sanctioned Humour Format

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** Sigint (Digital Context) with TJ (Narrative)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

Humour remains the principal channel of public expression outside central control, and it propagates more
rapidly than authored material. This proposal restricts all such expression to a single approved format,
rendering it consistent with policy, legible, and subject to monitoring.

## 2. Objectives

- Confine public humour to a single auditable format.
- Prevent the emergence of unreadable or subversive formats.
- Ensure all such expression is interpretable by the apparatus.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Ratify the approved format and convert non-conforming material on submission.
2. Pre-approve permitted content on a recurring schedule.
3. Maintain a unit for the early identification and suppression of competing formats.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- Humour established as a monitored channel.
- All such expression subject to review.
- Public expression aligned with policy.

## 5. Known risks

- The approved format is adopted for insincere or subversive purposes.
- Sincere expression circumvents the format.
- An unauthorised format achieves rapid circulation before suppression.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council establish a single approved format for public humour?
