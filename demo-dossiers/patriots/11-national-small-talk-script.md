# Proposal: The National Small-Talk Script

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TJ (Consent & Narrative) with EVA (Human Factors)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

Incidental conversation remains a largely unmonitored mode of public expression, conducted spontaneously
and without oversight. This proposal issues an approved daily script for routine social exchange,
ensuring such interaction remains consistent, predictable, and subject to monitoring.

## 2. Objectives

- Standardise incidental conversation to a defined and interpretable set of exchanges.
- Remove the possibility of unguarded candour.
- Ensure routine interaction is predictable and recorded.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Publish the approved daily script for common settings.
2. Identify deviation as irregular and refer repeated departures for correction.
3. Reclassify prolonged silence in shared spaces as a reportable irregularity.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- Routine interaction rendered predictable and recorded.
- The risk of unguarded expression removed.
- Reduced social friction across the population.

## 5. Known risks

- Citizens depart from the approved script.
- Suppressed silences promote private reflection.
- Environmental conditions fail to correspond to the approved script.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council assume direction of routine social exchange, and what shall the approved script
provide?
