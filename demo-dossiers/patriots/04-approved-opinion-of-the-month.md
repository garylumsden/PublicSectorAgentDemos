# Proposal: The Approved Opinion of the Month

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TJ (Consent & Narrative)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

The population exhibits a sustained demand for disagreement. This proposal satisfies that demand within
controlled limits by designating, each month, a single sanctioned matter of opinion on which the public
may hold and express divergent views. Permitted disagreement is confined to matters of no consequence.

## 2. Objectives

- Provide a credible simulation of open discourse.
- Direct the public appetite for disagreement toward pre-approved, low-consequence matters.
- Ensure that all consequential matters remain outside permitted debate.

## 3. Mechanism

1. The council designates a monthly matter of controversy and prepares the supporting positions for each
   side.
2. Public expression of the sanctioned disagreement is encouraged.
3. Citizens are commended for their independence of thought.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- A convincing impression of open discourse at minimal cost.
- A population that considers itself heard.
- Dissent contained within a closed and decorative channel.

## 5. Known risks

- A designated matter acquires unintended consequence.
- Citizens advance opinions outside the sanctioned matter.
- A designated matter proves unexpectedly divisive and produces genuine social friction.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council license the month's single permitted matter of disagreement, and what shall it
be?
