# Proposal: Operation Eternal Sunset

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** EVA (Optics) with TJ (Narrative)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

Distressing imagery agitates the population and promotes independent reflection. This proposal replaces
all imagery accompanying public information with approved landscape photography, separating events from
their visual representation and maintaining public composure.

## 2. Objectives

- Reduce collective agitation across the population.
- Separate the occurrence of events from their visual depiction.
- Ensure public information is presented in a uniformly composing manner.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Pair every item of public information, irrespective of gravity, with an approved landscape image.
2. Rotate the approved imagery to prevent recognition of the practice.
3. Reclassify any genuinely informative image as distressing and substitute approved material.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- A composed and compliant population.
- Public information rendered visually neutral.
- Reduced attachment of public sentiment to specific events.

## 5. Known risks

- Minor irregularities in the imagery are interpreted as significant.
- The population grows suspicious of consistently favourable imagery.
- Substituted imagery acquires unintended significance.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council adopt approved landscape imagery across all public information channels?
