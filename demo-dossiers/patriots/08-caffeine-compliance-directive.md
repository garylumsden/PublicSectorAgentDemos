# Proposal: The Caffeine Compliance Directive

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TR (Force) with EVA (Logistics)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

The collective mood and tempo of the population can be regulated through controlled management of a
substance already in widespread daily use. This proposal places the global supply of coffee under central
management, permitting the regulation of collective alertness in accordance with policy requirements.

## 2. Objectives

- Regulate the collective energy of the population as a managed variable.
- Raise alertness in advance of required activity and reduce it in advance of unrest.
- Establish public mood as an instrument of policy.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Mandate a single approved product; meter supply by region and by time.
2. Distribute decaffeinated product as a moderating measure in districts exhibiting unrest.
3. Maintain a reserve for periods of required heightened activity.

## 4. Anticipated benefits

- Collective mood managed as a measurable resource.
- Unrest moderated by region before it develops.
- A compliant population that regards the arrangement as its own choice.

## 5. Known risks

- Withdrawal produces agitation among otherwise compliant citizens.
- Disclosure of substitution undermines public confidence.
- Alternative suppliers and substances emerge outside central control.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council place the collective supply under central management, and is moderated product
an acceptable instrument of control?
